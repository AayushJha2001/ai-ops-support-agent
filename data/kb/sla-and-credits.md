# SLA and Service Credits

## Uptime commitment
Enterprise plans include a 99.95% monthly uptime SLA. Business plans include a 99.9% SLA. Starter and Team plans have no contractual SLA.

## Service credits
If monthly uptime falls below the SLA, eligible customers receive service credits: 10% of the monthly fee for uptime below the SLA but at least 99.0%, 25% for uptime between 95.0% and 99.0%, and 50% for uptime below 95.0%.

## How to claim
Credits must be requested within 30 days of the end of the affected month by opening a ticket in the "billing" category with the incident dates. Credits are applied to the next invoice; they can also be paid out as a partial refund on request.

## Exclusions
Scheduled maintenance announced at least 48 hours in advance, customer-caused outages and force majeure events are excluded from SLA calculations.
