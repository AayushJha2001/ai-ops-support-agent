# Support Escalation Policy

## When the assistant must escalate to a human
The assistant hands the conversation to a human support specialist when: the customer explicitly asks for a human; the request involves legal threats, chargebacks or regulatory complaints; there is a suspected security breach or account compromise; the customer reports a production outage on a Business or Enterprise plan; a refund above 500 USD is requested; or the assistant cannot resolve the issue after several attempts.

## Severity
Critical: security breach, data loss or full production outage. High: production impact, legal threats, chargebacks. Medium: billing disputes and repeated failed resolutions. Low: customer simply prefers a human.

## Response targets
Critical escalations are acknowledged within 15 minutes, high within 1 hour, medium within 4 business hours and low within 1 business day.

## What the customer is told
The customer is told that a specialist will follow up, given the ticket number and the expected response time for the severity.
