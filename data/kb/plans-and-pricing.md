# Plans and Pricing

## Available plans
Acme Cloud offers four plans. Starter costs 19 USD per month and includes 1 seat with community support. Team costs 79 USD per month and includes 5 seats with standard email support. Business costs 249 USD per month and includes 25 seats with priority support. Enterprise costs 999 USD per month and includes 100 seats, premium support with a named technical account manager, and a 99.95% uptime SLA.

## Annual billing
Paying annually gives a discount equivalent to two free months (you pay for 10 months and receive 12).

## Upgrades and downgrades
Upgrades take effect immediately and the price difference is prorated on the next invoice. Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period. Downgrading below the number of seats in use is not allowed until extra users are removed.

## Free trial
New accounts get a 14-day free trial of the Team plan. No payment method is needed during the trial. When the trial ends without a payment method the account moves to a read-only state for 30 days before deletion.
