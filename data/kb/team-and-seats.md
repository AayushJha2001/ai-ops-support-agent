# Team Members and Seats

## Inviting members
Admins invite members under Settings > Team > Invite. Invitations expire after 7 days. Each active member uses one seat.

## Adding seats
Extra seats beyond the plan allowance cost 12 USD per seat per month on Team and 9 USD on Business and Enterprise. Seats are billed prorated from the day they are added.

## Roles
Owner: full control including billing and deleting the account. Admin: manage members and settings but not billing. Member: use projects they are invited to. There is exactly one owner per account; ownership can be transferred under Settings > Team.

## Single sign-on (SSO)
SAML SSO is available on Business and Enterprise plans. SCIM user provisioning is available on Enterprise only.
