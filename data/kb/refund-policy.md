# Refund Policy

## Eligibility window
Monthly plans can be refunded within 14 days of the invoice date. Annual plans can be refunded within 30 days of the invoice date; after 30 days, annual plans receive a prorated refund for the unused full months only.

## What is not refundable
Usage-based overage charges, domain registrations and invoices older than 90 days are not refundable. Accounts suspended for abuse are not eligible for refunds.

## Partial refunds
Support can issue a partial refund for a service disruption that breached the SLA (see the SLA Credits article) or for duplicate charges. A partial refund cannot exceed the amount paid on the invoice minus any amount already refunded.

## Approval requirements
Every refund issued by the support assistant must be approved by a human support specialist before money is moved. Refunds above 500 USD must be handled by the billing team through a ticket and cannot be issued by the assistant.

## Timing
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days. The invoice status changes to "refunded" or "partially_refunded" immediately after approval.
