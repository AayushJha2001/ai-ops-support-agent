# Billing and Invoices

## Billing cycle
Invoices are issued on the same day each month (or year) as the subscription start date. Payment is due within 7 days of the invoice date.

## Failed payments and past-due accounts
If a payment fails we retry the card on days 1, 3 and 7 after the due date. During this period the subscription is marked "past_due" and the account keeps working. After 14 days past due the account is suspended until the balance is paid. Paying the open invoice reactivates the account within a few minutes.

## Updating the payment method
Account owners can update their card under Settings > Billing > Payment method. The assistant cannot see or store full card numbers; never share a full card number in chat.

## Duplicate charges
If you were charged twice for the same invoice, contact support with the invoice numbers. Duplicate charges are always refundable in full.

## Invoice disputes
To dispute an invoice, open a billing ticket with the invoice number and the reason. Disputed invoices are reviewed by the billing team within 3 business days and collection is paused during the review.

## Tax and VAT
Invoices include VAT for customers in the EU and UK unless a valid VAT number is saved on the account. Adding a VAT number does not change invoices issued before it was added.
