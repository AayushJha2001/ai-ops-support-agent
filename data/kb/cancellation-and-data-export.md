# Cancellation and Data Export

## How to cancel
The account owner can cancel under Settings > Billing > Cancel subscription. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing period; the account remains fully usable until then.

## Data retention after cancellation
After the subscription ends, data is kept in read-only mode for 30 days and then permanently deleted. Deleted data cannot be recovered by support.

## Exporting data
Use Settings > Data > Export to download all projects as a ZIP archive. Exports up to 10 GB are generated within one hour; larger exports are emailed as a link within 24 hours.

## Reactivation
A cancelled account can be reactivated at any time during the 30-day read-only period by choosing a plan.
