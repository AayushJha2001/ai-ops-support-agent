# Incidents and Service Status

## Status page
Live service status and incident history are published at status.acme-cloud.example. Customers can subscribe to email or webhook notifications there.

## Reporting an outage
If the status page shows no incident but your service is down, open a ticket in the "technical" category with your region, the affected project and the time the problem started. Outages affecting production for Business and Enterprise customers are treated as urgent.

## Incident severity levels
SEV1: full outage of a core service for many customers. SEV2: major feature degraded or a full outage for a single Enterprise customer. SEV3: minor feature degraded with a workaround available.

## Post-incident reports
For SEV1 and SEV2 incidents a public post-incident report is published within 5 business days.
